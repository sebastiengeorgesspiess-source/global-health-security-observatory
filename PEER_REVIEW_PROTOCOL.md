# Independent review protocol

Status: awaiting independent external review.

Reviewers should verify publisher and dataset identity, indicator definitions and units, country-code joins, temporal coverage, missing-value handling, notice deduplication, observed-versus-context separation, map legends, export consistency, medical-risk wording and reproducibility. No review status may be claimed until a qualified independent reviewer submits a signed report with expertise, conflicts, major issues, minor issues, recommendation and date.
